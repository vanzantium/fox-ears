## What changed

### (1) Tempo (`app/src/lib/hearing/tempo.ts`, `tempoHypotheses.ts`)

- If the top-scoring sub-grid peak is under `minComb`, fall back to the best-scoring peak that still clears the gate, instead of returning no tempo.
- If that winner (or any pulse) is above 180 BPM, fold by 2, 3 and 4 and grid-fit the slower candidates. In the 180–220 band, `/2` is preferred when the half sits in the usual 60–160 beat range, even if 8th-note onsets make the faster gridFit look stronger. 128, 135, and the rest of the 14-song displayed tempos were left alone (all ≤ 137.9).
- The 180 ms IOI floor is no longer a performed groove. Candidate generation may use IOIs down to 120 ms so 96-BPM sixteenths can vote; a 180 ms period is treated as an artifact and folded or skipped. Groove pick uses coverage / median error × prior (`gridFitQuality`), not raw inlier count.
- `preferred_for.unresolved` is true when displayed and felt disagree without a metric relation, when felt is still above 220, or when the display is blank but a dense-path groove is claimed. Keep Moving now reports grid=98.9, groove=98.9, unresolved=false.

### (2) Sections (`app/src/lib/hearing/form.ts`)

- Once tempo is locked, internal cuts snap to a 2-bar unit of an 8-bar pop schema (SOFT), using the first beat as phase when it is in the first bar.
- Cuts that fall through an ongoing vocal are moved to the nearest vocal gap (within 2 s) or dropped.
- Adjacent same-family sections with no vocal gap at the boundary merge. Chorus–chorus merges are capped at 12 bars so a chorus cannot swallow the next verse and chorus into a 40-bar block; verse family may merge up to 32 bars (a long second verse is legal). Chorus and verse still do not merge with each other; chorus and outro still do not merge.
- `section_schema` is attached to the chart.

### (3) Tuning pre-pass (`app/src/lib/hearing/tuning.ts`, wired in `score.ts`)

- Estimate cents off A440 from voiced `pitch_hz` (KDE mode, then a trimmed circular mean so octave errors do not collapse confidence).
- Report `{ cents_vs_440, a4_hz, confidence, applied, label: "SOFT", note }`.
- Apply the shift to chroma / bass chroma (fractional circular rotation) and remap midi / note with the estimated A4 only when `|cents| ≥ 12`, confidence ≥ 0.35, and at least 24 voiced frames. The 14-song set all sat under that gate (largest was stinky-skunk at +11.21 ¢, not applied). Keep Moving applied −24.25 ¢ (A4 ≈ 433.9 Hz). Key stayed D major.

`hzToMidi` in `pitch.ts` now accepts an optional `a4Hz` (default 440). Harness output (`harness/fox-calibrate.ts`) prints the A4 field and section schema.

## Tightening (Thursday 2026-11-19, evening MST, America/Dawson_Creek)

Taylor approved this tightening and a merge so others can run it ([pt81u]). Changes on the same branch, still against `86b78b6`:

1. **Missing chorus-2 cut.** After tempo is locked, a long stretch (16 bars or more) is scanned on the 8-bar grid. A cut is added when a vocal dip near the grid agrees with an energy jump or a peak just after the grid (SOFT schema check, `proposeGridCuts`). On Keep Moving this lands at 99.8 s. The mid-verse novelty cut at 58.5 s then merges back into verse 2.
2. **Roles.** `assignSectionRoles` no longer treats a prime (A') as a chorus cue. Recurrence of the first verse stays a verse even if it is louder than chorus 1. A later section that is the energy peak and chorus-length is still a chorus, even when one-key chroma gives it the verse letter.
3. **Tuning residual.** When at least 80 frames have pitch confidence ≥ 0.8, the estimate uses that subset. Keep Moving moved from A4 ≈ 433.9 Hz (−24.25 ¢) to **432.7 Hz (−28.99 ¢)**. Still SOFT; exact 432 is −31.8 ¢.
4. **750 ms click seed.** 750 ms is no longer always seeded as a click period. It is only added when sparse IOIs already show a near-80 BPM pulse (flux comb can still find a quiet click). Stinky-skunk no longer invents an 80 BPM click. Neon-whiskers still reports one (likely from IOI or flux, not the unconditional seed).

**Gates after tightening:** 14-song key **11/14**, tempo STRICT 10/14, FORGIVEN **10/14**, no displayed-tempo movement. Keep Moving stays a key and tempo hit, now with V/C/V/C/Outro. Section totals 178 → 137 (was 135 just before this tightening: 4-spiral_system 12→10, 6-pressure_makes 14→15, 7-involuntary_spiral 9→10, neon-whiskers 9→11). Overall still far below the pre-P3 over-split.

`7-involuntary_spiral` is still a key miss, but the name moved from F major (0.76) to C minor (0.72). The librosa stand-in is C major. This does not change the 11/14 score.
