### 1. Work / version identity

`app/src/lib/hearing/version.ts` and fixtures `app/fixtures/works/p1-versions.json`. A version has `work_id`, `version_id`, `audio_sha256`, `lyrics_source_id`, `relation_to_work` (`original` / `live` / `remix` / `cover` / `edit` / `stretch` / `unknown`), plus separate `measured`, `reported`, and `listener_reading` (Veyra) blocks.

Six records: Fox at the Gate master and 108 BPM stretch; Dreaming Like a Rocket Ship (Song A solo and Suno remix); Tired of Feeding the Lie (Song B solo and Suno remix).

### 2. Multi-pulse tempo

`app/src/lib/hearing/tempoHypotheses.ts`, wired through `buildChart` and the harness. A recording can carry a `reference_click`, a `performed_groove`, and a `subdivision_or_double`. `preferred_for` names a `transcription_grid` and a `felt_groove`, and sets `unresolved` when those disagree. Scoring is per role (`scoreTempoRole`, ±4%), so hearing the guitar is not a miss of a click task.

The click detector looks first for a tight regular pulse on sparse flux peaks, then (if the mix buries the metronome) on the denser onset list with a stricter error cap. A flux comb on the 10 ms tick stream is used to rank candidate periods so a quiet 80 BPM click can beat a louder ~83–84 strum. When the sub-grid locks on a double (~168) with a strong half (~84), the performed groove is the slower level. The scalar `tempo_bpm` field is unchanged.

**Song A / B results** (harness at default sensitivity 0.58, 2026-11-16, America/Dawson_Creek). Receipt: `docs/calibration/p1/song-ab-tempo.json`.

| Recording | Scalar tempo (unchanged) | reference_click | performed_groove | subdivision | preferred_for | Key (measured) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Song A solo | 168.1 (rival 84 at 91%) | **80** (109 inliers, median err 1.66 ms, conf 0.79) | **84** | 168 | grid 80, groove 84, **unresolved** | E major (0.94) |
| Song B solo | 80 (rival 160 at 62%) | **80** (99 inliers, median err 1.51 ms, conf 0.81) | **80** | 160 | grid 80, groove 80, resolved | E minor (0.73) |
| Suno remix of A | 167.5 (rival 83.5 at 97%) | none recovered (no metronome in the remake) | 83.5 | 167 | grid 83.5, groove 83.5 | E major (0.95) |
| Suno remix of B | 80 (rival 160 at 87%) | none recovered | 80 | 160 | grid 80, groove 80 | E minor (0.77) |

Song A is the case Veyra called out: the organ can now report the 80 click and the ~84 guitar groove as two real features, and score each role on its own. Song B’s click and groove agree at 80. The two Suno remixes were not expected to keep the click; they don’t, and that is recorded rather than forced.

### 3. Mix-vs-stem dual analysis (research, optional)

`app/src/lib/research/capabilities.ts` and `mixStem.ts`. Demucs / torchcrepe / faster-whisper sit behind a capability check. `baseStartupRequiresResearchTools()` is `false`. Essentia (AGPL) and madmom (CC BY-NC-SA) are optional only. `preferReading()` scores WER, token coverage, voiced fraction, logprob, and leakage; it never hard-codes stem or mix as the winner. Both receipts are kept. A drums-stem check on Song A still recovers 80 BPM at conf 0.88, which agrees with the new mix-side click hypothesis.

### 4. Read-rate-independent key / sections

Spectra are computed on a fixed 10 ms audio-clock grid (`SPECTRAL_GRID_MS`) with a time-constant pole `tau = -10 / ln(0.62) ≈ 21.07 ms` (`smoothPole`), not a per-read `smoothingTimeConstant`. This is in:

- the live engine (`app/src/lib/audio/engine.ts` — AnalyserNode smoothing is zeroed; the pole is applied in `snapshot()` with audio-clock `dt`);
- the offline harness (`harness/offline-engine.ts`, copied to the gitignored `app/calibration/` working copy);
- `buildChart`, which resamples frames onto the same 10 ms grid before key and form.

`--tick-hz` no longer changes the analyser hop. Jitter and background gaps can still skip reads; they do not change the smoothing pole.

**60 / 120 / 144 Hz proof** (local wavs, `FOX_EARS_P1_RATE_FILES=1`, 2026-11-16 4:19 PM America/Dawson_Creek). Receipt: `docs/calibration/p1/rate-equivalence.json`. Materially equivalent **within each file** at all three rates (identical key, confidence, section count, and cut times):

| File | Key at 60 / 120 / 144 | Sections | Cuts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fox at the Gate master | **G major** (0.87) at all three | 10 | identical (first cut 9805 ms, last outro 99805–108380 ms) |
| 108 BPM stretch | **D major** (0.87) at all three | 8 | identical (first cut 8732 ms, outro 88732–96290 ms) |

In P0 the stretch could read D major at 120 Hz and G at other rates. P1 makes the stretch **D major at every tested rate**. That is rate-stability, not a claim that D is the right key for the stretch (the master is G major). Sections still over-split; that is a later problem, not a display-rate one. A synthetic chord-changing fixture also agrees across 60/120/144 and runs in CI without audio files.

### 5. Section disagreement receipts

`app/src/lib/hearing/sectionCues.ts`. Vocal entry and lyric-repetition times are first-class cues. They do not silently rewrite novelty cuts. A disagreement receipt is emitted when a cue is more than the agreement window from the nearest cut. `hierarchicalForm` no longer calls `splitAtVocal` as a hidden override. On Song A the harness reported vocal entry at 24.81 s with the nearest novelty cut at 41.55 s (chorus).

### 6. Mode is a cue, not valence

`app/src/lib/hearing/modeReceipt.ts`, `affect.ts`, `songModel.ts`. `modeValenceContribution` is always 0; mean valence no longer moves when only the mode flips. A mode change emits a `REVIEW` / `changed-input` receipt (`cue: "mode"`) and keeps the first-listen appraisal quiet. `--compare` in the harness reports those receipts, not inward/release feeling flips. PR #5 / P0 documentation was corrected so it cannot be read as “all hearing is display-independent.”

### 7. Remix compare as a first-class object

`app/src/lib/hearing/remixCompare.ts` builds a compare object from two version records (and optional preferred mix/stem readings). Same `work_id` is required. Listener readings stay on the side. The earlier narrative compare in `docs/calibration/taylor-solo-2026-11-16/REMIX-COMPARE.md` (merged with PR #7) is unchanged.
