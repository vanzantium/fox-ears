## What changed (`app/src/lib/hearing/keyResolve.ts`)

1. **Test the third** (`testTheThird`). Once a tonic is chosen, compare the major and minor degrees above that tonic: 3 vs b3 (weight 1), 6 vs b6 (0.5), 7 vs b7 (0.25; 7 is shared with harmonic minor and b7 with Mixolydian, so it counts least). Frames whose bass sits on the tonic (home chord) get 3× weight; the last 4 s get +1. Chroma is first corrected for overtone leakage (3rd harmonic → +7 semitones, 5th harmonic → +4, both 0.12), because a loud tonic's 5th harmonic lands on the major third and pushes minor songs toward major. If the third clearly disagrees (|score| ≥ 0.12) and the parallel key is within 0.10 Pearson of the pick, the mode flips, with a note. If the third can't split a near tie (parallel within 0.05), the key is reported **UNRESOLVED** with a `parallel` hypothesis instead of a guess.
2. **Relative major/minor** (`relativeCheck`). Relative keys share all seven pitch classes, so the profile can't split them. When they're within 0.10, Fox Ears asks for "home" evidence: cadence bass, cadence chroma, bass share, V→I in the bass, and the harmonic-minor leading tone (7→1 of the minor, which is #5→6 of the major). A strong cadence or a 2-vote margin decides it. Otherwise the result is **UNRESOLVED** and both keys are kept (`global_key` plus `relative`).
3. **5 as impostor.** The existing rule stays (a dominant pedal is a clue, not a veto). The 4 vs #4 tie-breaker was already there (`uniqueDegreeVote`); it now **abstains** when the difference is tiny (< 0.006 of the profile) instead of casting a coin-flip vote.
4. **Borrowed chords vs modulation** (`borrowingVsModulation`). On bar windows (4 beats at the detected tempo, else 2 s): b3/b6/b7/#4 in a major key, or 3/6/#4 in a minor key, are logged as modal borrowing, with `tonic_held` when the bass stays on the tonic. A **modulation** is flagged only when a new tonic wins both the bass and the local key for at least 4 consecutive bars **and** that stretch uses a pitch class the old key doesn't own. A IV vamp with no new pitch class is not a modulation. Modulations are added as `section_key` hypotheses and never change the global key.
5. **Mode colour, SOFT only** (`modeTag`). Mixolydian (3 + b7 over 7) or Dorian (b3 + 6 over b6) is reported as `mode_tag` with label SOFT. It never changes the major/minor call.

`SongChart.key_detail` carries `third_test`, `relative`, `modulations`, `borrowing` and `mode_tag`. `key_status` can now be UNRESOLVED for tonic-vs-dominant, relative or parallel reasons. `buildChart` passes the bar length from the detected tempo.
